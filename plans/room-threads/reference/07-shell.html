Nothing in the interface survives four metres except one strip along the top edge of the window. So that strip carries the entire state, and it carries it in motion rather than colour.

THE NON-VISUAL SPECIFICATION · WHAT EACH MEDIUM CARRIES, AND WHAT IT MAY NEVER CARRY
A spoken claim carries its own uncertainty in grammar, not in a disclaimer. Verified things are said plainly; unverified things are said with their source attached.
"The auth tests pass" versus "Pike says the auth tests pass, I haven't run them myself." Both are one sentence, neither is a caveat, and a person half listening from across a room registers the difference without being taught it — because that is already how people report second-hand information.
