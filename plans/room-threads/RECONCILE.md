# Reconcile the design against the plan

The five-round reference supersedes the old four-screen `quiet-inbox` premise named by concerns 00, 03, and 10. This is a plan correction, not implementation scope.

## DECIDED BY THE DESIGN, MISSING FROM THE PLAN

1. **Autonomy is a durable, attributable rule system, not a UI preference.** A rule is the human's exact sentence; it has author(s), date, scope, invocation history, a reversible replacement/withdrawal operation, and is quoted at the point it decides work. It is replayed against prior evidence before a changed rule takes effect; agents must not silently stretch it. This includes the rule's applicability boundary and its outcome. `03-machinery.html` → 2a, 2b, 3b, 6a, 6b; `04-beyond.html` → 4a, 4b; `05-first-week.html` → 2a, 2b. **New concern: autonomy rules and provenance**, blocked by a schema expansion; concern 02 must emit rule-fired proof cards, and concern 10 must own their explanatory copy.

2. **There is an immutable non-delegatable boundary.** Credentials, spend, deletion, publishing/external release, and legal-edge decisions may never be widened by learned rules. A rule may propose its own scope only outside that boundary; the boundary itself is product policy. `04-beyond.html` → 3a, 3b, 5b; `05-first-week.html` → 1a, 2b. **New concern: delegation boundary enforcement**, with server-side authorization rather than a client label.

3. **Instructions require a recorded reading before action.** An agent returns its line-by-line interpretation, separates reversible work from irreversible work, records correction cost, and leaves irreversible parts pending. The design also settles one specific behavioural contract: an agent may object once, before work, with a falsifiable prediction; an overrule is not a fault, but the prediction outcome is retained. `03-machinery.html` → 1a, 1b; `05-first-week.html` → 6a, 6b. **New concern: instruction/readback and objection records**, attached to node work and projected through concern 02.

4. **Stillness is distinct from calm and is measured per plan.** The planner emits one conversational stall message—not a monitor—only when a non-parked plan exceeds its own observed normal from its own units. The model needs last meaningful movement, per-plan motion history/baseline, parked/intentional-still state, blocked cause, eligible successor count, and an explicit acknowledgement/park/drop outcome. `04-beyond.html` → 2a, 2b. **New concern: plan motion and stall evidence**; concern 04 can consume this data but cannot invent it.

5. **Work that leaves the app has a three-condition gate.** It may notify outside the app only when no rule can settle it, it blocks work that otherwise would move, and it can be answered in one sentence; it is delayed long enough for internal recovery. The originating human sentence and later “was it worth it?” review are part of the record. `04-beyond.html` → 1a, 1b. **New concern: external-notification gate and audit**, after autonomy rules and delegation enforcement.

6. **Cold start must be honest.** Six defaults are explicitly borrowed, individually reversible, and replaced by human sentences; the one non-defaultable question is out-of-hours contact. A visible unknowns ledger states what the product does not know, the evidence/sample needed, and the cost of not knowing. `05-first-week.html` → 1a, 1b. **New concern: learning state (borrowed defaults and unknowns)**; it supplies the provenance needed by autonomy, stalls, and agent records.

7. **Historical truth has fidelity, age, and loss semantics.** Decisions and their then-known evidence remain whole; compaction names what was cut and when, records who authorized it, and never pretends a summary is the full record. Handover retains author attribution, marks stale evidence, and requires re-verification against current main. `03-machinery.html` → 5a, 5b; `02-surfaces.html` → 4b, 8a, 8b. **New concern: retention, compaction, and handover provenance**; concern 06 must provide the current summary, not silently replace this archive model.

8. **People and agents have records, not scores.** Agent evidence needs sample size, role-default versus proven behaviour, openable source units, dated claims that can become stale/withdrawn, provisional onboarding/checking, and no cross-agent leaderboard. `03-machinery.html` → 4a, 4b; `05-first-week.html` → 4a, 4b, 5a, 5b. **New concern: evidence-backed agent records**, fed by the retained work history.

9. **Multi-human authority is first-class.** Questions route to one named accountable human; instructions retain author at every surface; rules remain distinct rather than becoming anonymous house policy; conflicts are a human disagreement, not an implicit merge. `05-first-week.html` → 2a, 2b. **New concern: human authority and rule conflict**, sharing the rule model.

10. **Plans are proposals before execution.** The planner records the original words, assumptions, proposed units/order/parallelism, and exact start consequences; ambiguous goals stop before node creation. `02-surfaces.html` → 7a, 7b. **Absorb into concern 02 plus a plan-proposal sub-concern**, because projection must distinguish proposed from started work.

11. **Interruption review, cost, and unwind are proof surfaces.** The system must preserve dependency-aware reversal cost, irreversibles versus nearest repair, spend versus waste with causes, and cost disclosure only when the decision changes it. `04-beyond.html` → 5a, 5b, 6a, 6b. **New concern: decision-impact evidence**, following durable rules/decision history.

## THE PLAN IS NOW WRONG

1. **Concern 01's merged `Node` schema is materially too narrow.** `src/nodes.ts` has one `parentId`, one `ownerId`, lifecycle state, goal, timestamps, and `channelId`; it cannot carry rule provenance/scope/invocation, instruction readings, decision alternatives/consequences, human authority, parked/stall evidence, evidence age, handover chain, retention fidelity, or immutable delegation class. This is not a cosmetic add-on: the design's behaviours need queryable, durable evidence. Blast radius: `Node`, `CreateNodeInput`, `NodeStore`, both stores/migrations, manager/server emitters, and node consumers/tests. The merged schema must be extended through associated records—not overloaded into optional `Node` fields—before concern 02 makes addressing permanent. Evidence: `src/nodes.ts`; `03-machinery.html` → 1a, 2a, 5b; `04-beyond.html` → 2a, 3a; `05-first-week.html` → 2a.

2. **Concern 03's coupled two-pane shell is superseded as the home-frame thesis.** The settled direction is a room home screen whose rail is a standing entrance and whose depth events project cards back into that room; selecting a plan in the rail does not make the main conversation cease to be the room narrative. The busy room explicitly shows cross-plan conversation beside a selected plan; detailed unit context is entered from the work/card. `DIRECTION.md` §What we are building; `01-room.html` → busy; `02-surfaces.html` → 3a, 8b. Reframe concern 03 around room shell + rail + doors, not “state pane and that node's conversation always coupled.”

3. **Concern 02's “everything else stays at its node” is too absolute.** The design requires manager-authored, unforgeable proof cards in the room for layer-2 events, while preserving local detail and suppressing noise. A `projectedNodeId` alone has neither event proof/provenance nor a defined room-projection policy. `DIRECTION.md` §What we are building; `01-room.html` → busy, quiet; `03-machinery.html` → 3b; `04-beyond.html` → 4a. Preserve the anti-firehose goal, but replace its binary upward-flow rule with explicit event classes and proof-card contracts.

4. **Concern 04 conflates activity ranking with the design's measured motion.** Velocity may order in-flight nodes, but the design's stall judgement is per-plan normal, explicitly excludes parked work, and becomes a planner message with causal facts and recovery choices. `04-beyond.html` → 2a, 2b. Concern 04 should remain ordering-only; extract stall detection into the new prerequisite concern.

5. **Concern 06's “regenerate, never append” is insufficient and partially misleading.** It covers live summaries, but the design intentionally preserves decisions/evidence and human text at full fidelity, compacts other material after an authorized policy, declares the cut, and never calls the result the record. `03-machinery.html` → 5a, 5b; `02-surfaces.html` → 4b. Split live regenerated summaries from immutable archive/compaction records.

6. **Concern 07 says the centre-pane model is unresolved; the design resolves the relevant question.** Room timeline is the default narrative; depth is entered through cards/rail, and selection must not yank a conversation mid-read. What remains open is exact router interaction, not whether the room is the home screen. `DIRECTION.md` §What we are building; `01-room.html` → busy; `02-surfaces.html` → 8b. Remove the obsolete “global feed versus coupled panes” fork before shell work.

7. **Concern 10 is necessary but underscoped.** Copy needs source facts not presently emitted: rule sentence/author/date, evidence age and staleness action, cut/fidelity, decision consequence, blast radius, and evidence-backed uncertainty. A string lint cannot recover facts absent from the event contract. `03-machinery.html` → 1a, 5b; `04-beyond.html` → 4a, 5b; `05-first-week.html` → 4a. Make concern 10 depend on the new evidence records and define typed proof-card payloads.

## STILL GENUINELY OPEN

- **Rule evaluation semantics:** the reference establishes human sentences and non-stretching, not the parsing/evaluation mechanism, conflict precedence across scopes, or retention/privacy boundaries for rule text. Settle with a technical prototype over real historical instructions and an explicit authority matrix. `03-machinery.html` → 6b; `05-first-week.html` → 2b.
- **Statistical thresholds:** “more likely than not,” sufficient sample, and plan-normal calculation are deliberately evidenced but not numerically specified. Settle from retained unit histories and a false-positive/false-negative review, not a global configured threshold. `04-beyond.html` → 2a; `05-first-week.html` → 4a, 5b.
- **External action adapters:** the product boundary identifies credentials, spend, deletion, publishing, and legal-edge work; it does not define provider-specific enforcement or an authority handoff for each integration. Settle per integration before connecting a real side effect. `04-beyond.html` → 3a, 5b.
- **Multi-parent work graph:** the design shows historical handover and plan/unit addressability, but does not choose primary-home references versus true multi-homing for a shared unit/PR. Retain the narrow part of concern 07 and decide it with real `BLOCKED_BY`/landing examples. `02-surfaces.html` → 8b; `07-navigation-model.md` §Approach 2.

## SEQUENCING

Before concern 02 proceeds beyond its current flight, amend its event contract and introduce the durable evidence model: associated rule, authority, instruction-readback, decision/evidence-age, handover, and plan-motion records; do not retrofit them after node-addressed cards spread. Correct concern 01's schema/migrations first, because its merged shape cannot represent those contracts. Keep FileStore/DbStore parity.

Before concern 03 begins, replace the obsolete coupled-pane premise with the room-first shell contract; decide only the remaining multi-parent router mechanics. Concern 04's ranking can follow node state, but stall detection must precede any UI that claims calm/still status. Concern 06 can proceed only as live summaries after archive/compaction semantics are separated.

After that foundation: autonomy-rule execution, delegation enforcement, external-notification gating, cost/unwind evidence, cold-start learning, and agent records can land in dependency order. Concerns 08 and 09 remain independently useful. Concern 10 must ship with each new proof emitter, not as a final copy pass.
