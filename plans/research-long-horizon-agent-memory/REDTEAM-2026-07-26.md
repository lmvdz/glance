## What the feedback missed

- C9's premise (labels don't defuse stale memory) is an **inference** from 2607.10608's
  placement-independence result — the paper never tested explicit staleness labels. That is a
  sharper caveat than any of the five points, and it is exactly why C9 is a pre-registered
  ablation rather than settled law.
- The "second promotion path" frame invites bolt-on complexity; the cleaner generalization is one
  principle (evidence-gated promotion) with two evidence kinds and one hard guard (models cannot
  self-authorize).
